Synthetic order and invoice record

1. Agreed order
The agreed order is for twelve chairs at $20 each, for a total of $240.
The agreed delivery deadline is June 10.

2. Invoice discrepancy
The invoice lists twelve chairs at $20 each but states a total of $260.
It also lists June 12 as the delivery date, contrary to the agreed order.
The invoice total and delivery date are disputed. No tax or delivery fee is
listed that would explain the difference between $240 and $260.

3. Status and limitations
The supplied record does not establish that the invoice was accepted as correct.
It does not establish that any payment was made or that delivery occurred.
The invoice entries do not establish an agreed change to the order total or
delivery deadline. No resolution of the dispute is recorded here.
